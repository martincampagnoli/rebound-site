Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Rebound Tracker ("Rebound") shows your Claude usage limits on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

What Rebound handles

  • Your claude.ai session. When you sign in to claude.ai inside Rebound, the app keeps the session cookie so it can read your usage. It is stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch and is sent only to claude.ai.
  • Your usage figures. Session and weekly usage percentages and reset times are fetched from claude.ai and cached on your devices so the app and complications can show them.

Advertising

The iPhone app shows a small ad at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect device and usage information such as your IP address, approximate location, device model and ad interactions. Rebound does not ask to track you across other apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), so it does not access your advertising identifier.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Rebound asks for your consent before showing ads, and you can change your choice any time with Privacy choices at the bottom of the screen. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your claude.ai session and your usage figures are never shared with Google or any advertiser.

What Rebound does not do

  • It does not send your claude.ai session or usage to the developer or any third party.
  • It has no analytics, and it does not track you across other apps and websites.
  • It does not read your conversations.

Purchases

The optional "Buy us a coffee" tip is processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Signing out

Tap ⋯ → Sign out on iPhone to delete the session from your iPhone and Apple Watch. Deleting the app also removes all of its data.
