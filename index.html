Rebound

Your Claude limits, on your wrist.

Rebound shows how much of your Claude subscription you have used, so you always know when you can keep going and when your limits come back.

  • Current session: your 5-hour usage and when it resets
  • This week: your 7-day usage and when it resets
  • Apple Watch: an app and complications for every face

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Open an issue and we'll get back to you.

Watch not updating? Open Rebound on your iPhone with your watch nearby to resend your session, then open Rebound on the watch.

Signed out or usage missing? Tap ⋯ → Sign out on iPhone and sign in to Claude again.

Privacy

Rebound has no accounts, analytics or ads. Your claude.ai session stays on your devices and is only sent to claude.ai. Read the privacy policy.
